Services

Land clearing

Cedar (Ashe juniper) and brush removal matched to your land and how you use it. Cedar clearing is where most owners start.

  • Cedar clearing, mulched in place
  • Pasture and larger-acreage reclamation, including stacking
  • Selective homesite and view clearing
  • Fence-line and firebreak clearing around structures
  • Follow-up regrowth maintenance

Typical scope: Cedar is cut below the lowest green limb and mulched on site. Oaks and hardwoods you want kept go on a save list.

How the work is done: We walk the property with you, mark what stays, and match the method to the density and terrain. Mulching leaves the ground covered, with no burning.

What you get: Cleared ground with the trees you chose still standing, plus before and after photos of the work.

Site and pad work

Grading and rough site prep for the places you plan to build.

  • Pads for homes, shops, barns and slabs
  • Homesite clearing and grading
  • Drainage shaping
  • Base material for pads and drives

Typical scope: Clear, rough-grade and shape the pad so water moves away from the building area.

How the work is done: Work is laid out at the site walk so pad location, access and drainage are agreed before equipment moves.

What you get: A prepared pad ready for your builder, surveyor or slab crew.

Roads

Ranch and entry roads, built new or repaired, for everyday truck and trailer traffic.

  • New ranch and entry roads
  • Grade-and-crown native roads
  • Base-built roads with compacted flex base or caliche
  • Culverts, crossings and drainage turnouts
  • Regrading of existing roads, including after storms

Typical scope: Clear the corridor, grade and crown, build drainage, and add base and surfacing where the road calls for it.

How the work is done: We plan the route and drainage on site and coordinate local operators with the right equipment, with one point of contact for you.

What you get: A drivable, drained road suited to the traffic you expect.

Barns

Barn, shop and outbuilding projects coordinated from site prep through completion.

  • Pole barns and post-frame barns
  • Equipment sheds and hay barns
  • Shops and outbuildings
  • Shell, shell with slab, or finished options

Typical scope: Site prep and pad, then the building itself, built by vetted builders. Electrical and plumbing are done by licensed trades.

How the work is done: We coordinate the site work and the trades so you are not managing several crews yourself.

What you get: A finished building on a properly prepared site, with one person to call throughout.

Ranch management

Ongoing care for your land, handled for you. A good fit for owners who live out of town.

  • Scheduled property visits with photo reports
  • Gate, fence, road and water checks
  • Brush control and regrowth follow-up
  • Coordination of vendors and repairs
  • Photo and activity records for wildlife or ag valuation upkeep

Typical scope: Recurring visits and upkeep tailored to your property, from basic watch-over to fuller care.

How the work is done: We visit on a schedule, document what we see, and arrange the work that needs doing.

What you get: Photo reports, a single point of contact, and records you can use. You file any tax paperwork yourself, and we give no tax advice.

Scope is confirmed at a site walk.
