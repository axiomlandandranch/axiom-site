Services

Land clearing

Cedar and brush removal done to fit the land and how you use it. We can clear selectively, leaving oaks and shade trees standing.

  • Cedar and brush removal
  • Selective clearing around oaks and shade trees
  • Brush pile burning or hauling where permitted
  • Fence-line and pasture clearing

Site and pad work

Grading and rough site prep for the places you plan to build. We shape the ground so water moves away from it.

  • Graded pads for homes, shops, barns and slabs
  • Drainage shaping
  • Rough site prep
  • Base material

Roads

Ranch and entry roads, built new or repaired. Built to carry everyday truck and trailer traffic.

  • Ranch and entry roads built or repaired
  • Drainage and culverts where needed
  • Base and surfacing for truck and trailer traffic

Barns

Barn, shop and outbuilding projects coordinated from site prep through completion. We work with the trades involved so you have one point of contact.

  • Barns and shops
  • Outbuildings
  • Site prep through completion
  • Coordination with the trades involved

Ranch management

Ongoing care for your land, handled for you. A good fit for owners who live out of town.

  • Brush control
  • Road and fence upkeep
  • Seasonal checks
  • Single point of contact for out-of-town owners
